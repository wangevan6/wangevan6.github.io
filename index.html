About

Learning models that connect perception, actions, and the physical world.

I am a PhD student in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Weinan E. My research interests include embodied AI, robot learning, world models, and AI agents.

Previously, I studied biostatistics at New York University and applied mathematics at the University of Waterloo.

Research interests

I am interested in how agents learn from interaction and use models of the world to reason about actions. My current focus is world action models for embodied intelligence.
